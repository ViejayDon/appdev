## *The Amazing Spider-Man* (2012)

***The Amazing Spider-Man*** is a 2012 superhero film starring **Andrew Garfield** as Peter Parker / Spider-Man and directed by **Marc Webb**. Launching a fresh reboot of the Spider-Man film series, it leans into Peter's scientific curiosity, his parents' disappearance, and his romance with Gwen Stacy.

### Story & Key Elements

Peter Parker uncovers a clue tied to his late father's research at Oscorp, leading him to **Dr. Curt Connors** and a bite from a genetically altered spider. After tragedy strikes Uncle Ben, Peter builds mechanical web-shooters and takes on the vigilante identity of **Spider-Man** as Connors's cross-species experiments spin out of control.

* **Mechanical Web-Shooters:** Peter engineers wrist-mounted shooters (`webShooter`) rather than relying on organic webbing.
* **Central Relationship:** Emma Stone plays **Gwen Stacy**, whose dynamic with Peter anchors much of the film's emotional core.
* **Main Antagonist:** Dr. Curt Connors transforms into **The Lizard** after testing his cross-species serum on himself.

1. Discover Richard Parker's research notes and visit Oscorp.
2. Gain spider-like powers and experience the loss of Uncle Ben.
3. Confront The Lizard to protect New York City.
